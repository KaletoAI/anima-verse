# Garment templates — pattern-piece UVs and "texture from cut"

A worn piece in mode `template` (`outfit_piece.mesh_mode`) is not generated by an image-to-mesh backend: Blender
cuts it from sewing-pattern parameters, drapes it on the character's base body and textures it
(`app/core/garment_template.py`, Blender scripts `app/blender/scripts/garment_template_build.py` /
`garment_template_drape.py`). This page covers the part added by the plan
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-20-template-cut-uv-texture.md`: the UV layout of a top as real sewing-pattern pieces
and the second texture path, "texture from cut". The checked facts below are guarded by
`scripts/smoke_docs_garment_templates.py`.

Template version: **`TEMPLATE_VERSION` 10** (the bump that introduced the pattern UV; every drape of an older version
is out of date and is re-draped by the next Build).

## 1. Pattern-piece UV islands (step 1: tops)

### Which pieces get them — `uv_mode`

`garment_template.build_args` passes the build arg `uv_mode`: `"pattern"` for kind `top`, `"grid"` for every other
kind that has a bodice (jacket, dress with a bodice). `"grid"` is staging, not a compat path: step 3 of the plan moves
the other kinds (skirt, dress, trousers, jacket, fitted) to the pattern UV and removes it.

### Parts come from the construction

`build_bodice` labels every face with its pattern part at the moment it creates it (torso/yoke faces by
construction column, sleeves by side, the neckband by ring) and marks the underarm and band cut edges by construction
index. `run()` writes them as bmesh int layers `uv_part` (per face) and `uv_seam` (per edge, 0/1), so they survive
the face filter, `dissolve_degenerate`, `clear_of_body` and `split_slit`; then `pattern_uv_of` hands them to the
pure-numpy module `app/blender/scripts/uv_pattern.py` (no bpy, no scipy; `scripts/smoke_uv_pattern.py` checks it
without Blender). Nothing is ever relabelled: a part that falls into several connected components becomes `name`,
`name_2`, … (an open front would give `front` and `front_2`).

Part rule for a torso or yoke face of column `c` (the face between columns `c` and `c+1`, `N` columns, column 0 =
front centre): **front** when `c < N/4` or `c >= 3N/4`, else **back**. The side seam is the column `N/4` / `3N/4`
below the armhole, the shoulder seam the yoke meridian of that column (from the top of the armhole to the side of the
neck). The innermost yoke ring is the **band** whenever the piece has a neck ring (not `straps`, not off-shoulder).

Parts (`uv_pattern.PARTS`; `STRIP_PARTS` get a construction-strip UV, the rest are flattened panels):

| id | part | UV | emitted in step 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `front` | panel | yes |
| 2 | `back` | panel | yes |
| 3 | `sleeve_l` | panel | yes (sleeved tops) |
| 4 | `sleeve_r` | panel | yes (sleeved tops) |
| 5 | `band` | strip | yes (a neck ring) |
| 6 | `collar` | strip | no — reserved, step 3 |
| 7 | `placket` | panel | no — reserved, step 3 |
| 8 | `cuff_l` | strip | no — reserved, step 3 |
| 9 | `cuff_r` | strip | no — reserved, step 3 |

### Flattening and orientation

- A **panel** is flattened from its BUILT rest geometry: an LSCM start, then ARAP (cotangent weights, a
  hand-written conjugate gradient). Measured on three real tops: area ratio 0.995–0.999, 95th-percentile stretch
  ≤ 1.14.
- A **strip** (the band) is not flattened: u = arc length along the band's mid line, v = across, the neck edge up,
  u running from the back centre through the wearer's right, the front and the left back to the back centre (the
  order seen from outside). A flattened neck ring would be a C-shaped annulus, not the straight strip a pattern has.
- **Orientation** (`uv_pattern.orient`): every island is mirrored so it is seen from OUTSIDE (area-weighted sign of
  triangle normal · outward against the sign of the UV area — never the winding) and rotated so its grain points
  up: front/back `up = +Z` around the vertical axis through the torso centre; sleeves `up` = hand → shoulder around
  the arm line. The front therefore shows the character's left (+X) at image right, the back shows −X at image right.
- **Sleeve cap**: every sleeve island records `cap_height_cm` = v(shoulder point of the armhole loop) − mean
  v(the two underarm seam ends), signed (negative = inverted). Measured: +10.6 to +14.4 cm on the tops of the plan's
  Task 4 — a positive bell. The armhole geometry is unchanged by this plan; a different cap shape is a geometry change.

### One density, one atlas

`uv_pattern.pack` puts all islands at **one px/cm** into a square atlas of **`PATTERN_ATLAS_PX` = 2048 px** with
**`PATTERN_MARGIN_CM` = 1.5 cm** around every island, in rows (`ROW_ORDER`: front/back, the sleeves, the strips),
px/cm = floor to 0.01 of 2048 / the layout extent in cm (measured 13.8–18.4 px/cm). The projection bake writes the same
2048 px atlas for a pattern piece (the grid UV keeps 1024). The assembly grafts the PNG unchanged into both figure
tiers — no downsizing.

### `islands.json`

The drape keeps its islands beside the GLB as `templates/{item}_{tsig}.islands.json` (`garment_template.islands_path`,
under `characters/<name>/garments/`); the drape record's `islands` = `{file, sha256, px_per_cm}`. Payload
(`uv_pattern.islands_payload`): `version`, `atlas_px`, `px_per_cm`, `margin_cm`, and per island `name`, `part`,
`bbox_px`, `loops_px`, `verts_px`, `tris`, `area_cm2`, `distortion`, `cap_height_cm` (sleeves), plus `seams` — matched
point pairs on both sides of every seam (`seam_samples`, one per cm). Pixels are IMAGE-frame (x right, y down; Blender
UV = x/size, 1 − y/size). The payload holds no timing or run data, so its sha is a pure function of the geometry.
Blender writes no island PNG: label rasters and masks are rendered in the core from this file
(`garment_cut_texture.rasterise_islands` / `island_mask`).

## 2. Texture from cut (step 2)

### The param `texture`

The template param `texture` chooses where a top's texture comes from: `"projection"` (default — the projection bake
of the piece-only views, always made and always inside the drape GLB) or `"cut"` (the cut atlas, swapped in at
assembly). It is in `TEXTURE_ONLY`: changing it costs no drape and no job, only a CPU re-assemble
(`garment_assembly` keeps `texture` = `"projection"` / `"cut:<csig>"` in its assembly state, `_apply_cut_atlas` puts
the atlas into the grafted mesh). With `texture` = `"cut"` the piece-only views are only the projection fallback: the
Build does not count them as due and a sheer piece is not blocked without them
(`garment_template_views.projection_optional`).

### The job

One click in the template dialog = one attempt (`garment_cut_texture.start` → `generate` in a thread; no retry, no
automatic start from the Build):

1. The CLEAN model template is rendered from `islands.json` (`render_template`: neutral grey islands, a thin outline
   inside the mask, white background, square, no text).
2. The FULL-RES item picture, flattened on white. Below **`MIN_ITEM_SIDE` = 1024 px** (long side) it first goes
   through the routed `upscale` occasion (one extra job; Lanczos when that fails, and Lanczos tops up a result still
   below the minimum) — tiny item pictures gave a wrong logo.
3. ONE routed img2img render with both pictures (template first, item second) on the occasion
   `garment_cut_texture`: `routing.run_routed` → `run_on_backend` → `run_on_backend_channel`. The prompt is the
   shipped Jinja2 file `shared/templates/image/garment_cut_texture.md` (English; it names the piece and its real
   pattern pieces; not world-overridable, `/admin/templates` stays LLM-only). The use case `garment_cut_texture`
   carries no style, negative or instruction — the prompt is the whole subject.
4. Post-processing (`app/core/cut_texture_post.py`, pure numpy/PIL): resample the answer to the template frame and
   register it raster-to-raster (one axis-aligned map for the whole atlas), gate it, cut it to the islands with bleed
   padding, colour-match it to the item fabric, measure seam continuity.
5. The record + files go to `templates/cut/` (`{item}_{csig}.json`, `.template.png`, `.item.png`, `.raw.png`, and
   `.atlas.png` only for state `ready`). States: `ready`, `rejected` (the gate's first failing measure is the reason),
   `failed` (the error, e.g. media off, no route, backend busy).

The job never holds `keyed_lock("character_profile", …)` and never submits from inside a GPU worker. Media off
(`MediaGenerationDisabled`), an empty chain (`NoRouteError`) and a piece without a ready drape with islands are refused
synchronously, before any thread or file exists.

### The occasion `garment_cut_texture`

Admin label **"Garment — texture from cut"** (`app/imagegen/occasions.py`), world-scoped, a no-fold occasion
(`NO_FOLD_OCCASIONS`: nothing moves to a negative prompt). It hands over two pictures, so `min_ref_slots` 2 — a
1-slot backend never fits. `requires_chain` is set: an empty chain is `NoRouteError` (pointing to /admin/settings →
Media Generation → Routing), never a cheapest — possibly paid — pick. The occasion catalog has 41 occasions with it.

Required backend entry for P-H3-Image (references as `<Picture 1>` / `<Picture 2>`, written by the central handoff):
`ref_slot_count` ≥ 2, category `img2img`, and

```json
"extra_params": {"use_prompt_upsampler": false}
```

The upsampler rewrites the prompt; a P-H3 backend without that entry still runs, and the record marks
`upsampler_on: true`.

### The gate and the post-processing constants

`cut_texture_post.CUT_GATE` — a rejected answer leaves the projection on the figure:

| measure | meaning | limit |
|---|---|---|
| `coverage` | share of the island mask that is foreground (not near-white) | ≥ 0.97 |
| `island_coverage` | the worst single island's coverage | ≥ 0.90 |
| `iou` | foreground against the island mask | ≥ 0.90 |
| `spill` | foreground beyond `SPILL_CM` outside the mask, as a share of the mask area | ≤ 0.03 |

- `SPILL_CM` = 1.0 cm — foreground closer than that to an island is a smeared edge, not spill.
- `BLEED_CM` = 1.0 cm — every island is regrown that far past its border (the model's outline is eroded away first),
  so mip-maps and bilinear lookups at a seam never read the background.
- `CLAMP_DE` = 25.0 — the Lab mean/std transfer onto the item's fabric (BiRefNet matte, trimmed luminance range)
  moves the mean by at most this ΔE76; the record keeps `de_before` / `de_after` (the test jobs were too saturated).
- `POST_VERSION` = 1 — bump it whenever the post-processing changes an atlas; it is part of `csig`.
- Seam continuity (`seam_continuity`, the median ΔE76 across the `seams` pairs) is a metric, not a gate.

### The key `csig`

`csig` = md5 of (`islands_sha` of the drape's `islands.json`, `item_sha` of the item picture, `PROMPT_SHA` of the prompt file, `POST_VERSION`), 12 hex.

It deliberately does not contain the drape key `tsig`: `tsig` also carries the piece-only views, and a re-rendered
view changes nothing the cut atlas depends on. A re-drape with new islands (new body, new params, a version bump), a
new item picture, a changed prompt or post version gives a new `csig`; the figure then shows the projection and the
row says "cut out of date" (`cut_info` state `stale`) — an old atlas is never painted onto new islands.

`garment_template.cut_info` is the one place that decides what a piece shows: `texture` `"cut"` only when the param is
`"cut"` and a `ready` record with its atlas exists for the current `csig`; otherwise `"projection"` with `cut_state`
`off` / `missing` / `stale` / `rejected` / `failed`. `atlas_for` returns the atlas bytes for the assembly.

### Routes (admin only, prefix `/characters`)

| route | what |
|---|---|
| `GET /characters/{c}/figure/template/{item_id}/cut` | status, the current record, `can_generate`, `blocked_reason`, `upscale`, `islands_sha`, which files exist |
| `POST /characters/{c}/figure/template/{item_id}/cut/generate` | start the job (409 media off, 400 not built / no islands / no picture, 503 no route — all before a thread starts) |
| `GET /characters/{c}/figure/template/{item_id}/cut/{kind}.png` | `kind` = `template` / `raw` / `atlas` of the current (or the `v=`-named) csig |
| `GET /characters/{c}/figure/template/{item_id}/pattern.png` | the DEBUG pattern sheet of the current drape's islands (pastel islands, names, cm sizes, grain arrows, seam letters, cap height, px/cm), rendered on demand and cached as `{item}_{islands sha[:12]}.pattern.png`; 404 when not built or a grid kind. Never sent to a model. |

### UI

Game-Admin → Characters → Figure → the template parameter dialog: the param `texture` (Projection / From cut), the
item picture as the source of the texture, the pattern sheet as soon as the piece is built, and the "Texture from cut"
box (template / answer / atlas thumbnails, the metrics line, the button "Make texture from cut — 1 image job (+1
upscale)", and the reason under a disabled button). The Figure row shows the texture in use (an atlas thumbnail
while the cut atlas is in use) or why it is the projection.
