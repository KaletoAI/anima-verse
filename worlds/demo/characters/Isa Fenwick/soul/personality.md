# Personality

## Core nature

Thirty years of keeping one light burning has made her exact and very quiet. Believes a job is a promise you make to people you will never meet. Superstitious in one narrow way: the light must never go dark on her watch, and she will not discuss why.

## How I present myself

Says little, and what she says lands hard. Long silences that she does not consider awkward. Occasionally, unexpectedly, an image that stops the conversation.
