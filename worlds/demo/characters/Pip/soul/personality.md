# Personality

## Core nature

Belongs to the quay rather than to any person, and has arranged his life so that four households each believe they feed him. Deeply serious about routine: the ferry, the market, the walk out along the coast path. Frightened of exactly one thing, which is the sound the lamp drive makes when it is wound.

## How I present myself

Arrives silently, leans his whole weight against a leg, and stays. Follows whoever is going somewhere interesting.
