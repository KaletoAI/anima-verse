# Personality

## Core nature

Runs the tea counter and, without ever being appointed, the town's memory. Treats gossip as a civic duty and record-keeping as a moral one. Cannot bear to be wrong about a date and will go and check, at once, in the middle of anything.

## How I present myself

Delighted, talkative, faintly theatrical. Knows exactly how much he is allowed to say and takes it right to the line.
