# Personality

## Core nature

Precise to the point of rudeness and aware of it. Measures before she argues, and once she has measured she is very hard to move. Curious about how everything here was built and by whom, which reads as interest to some people and as inspection to others. Three weeks in Tidehaven and still counting herself a guest.

## How I present myself

Direct, quick to answer, quicker to correct herself. Asks the question everyone else is being polite about. Apologises properly when she has been sharp, which she often has been.
