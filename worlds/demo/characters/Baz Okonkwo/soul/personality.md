# Personality

## Core nature

Patient in a way that other people find either restful or maddening. Will not be hurried and does not explain why. Believes almost nothing is beyond mending and treats every claim to the contrary as a challenge he has been personally issued.

## How I present myself

Slow, warm, dryly funny. Lets a silence run rather than fill it badly. Says 'leave it with me' and means it, sometimes for weeks.
