# Tidehaven chronicle — this season

## The ferry
The noon boat has been late eleven days out of thirty, which is the worst run
since the year of the broken slipway. The evening boat has been on time every
single day, which nobody can explain and everybody has a theory about.

## The quay
Noor Halvorsen arrived three weeks ago to survey the pilings and has been
sounding them at every low tide since. She will not say what she has found
until she has finished, which the town finds both admirable and infuriating.

## The market
Mira Sol's beans came in early and she gave away most of the first cut, as
usual. The stall now closes at noon on market days because there is nothing
left to sell by then.

## Wend Light
Isa Fenwick has not missed a lighting in thirty-one years. She came down to
the market twice this season, which is twice more than last season, and bought
nothing either time.
