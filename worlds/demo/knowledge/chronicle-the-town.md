# Tidehaven — how the town works

## The terraces
The town stands on two cut terraces above the shore. The harbour road climbs
from Salt Quay to Market Terrace and on to the houses on the second terrace.
The coast path leaves the market to the south-west and runs out along the
headland to Wend Light — about forty minutes on foot, more against the wind.

## The repair nook
Anything broken goes to the nook before it goes anywhere else. Baz Okonkwo
runs it. The standing rule is that nothing is declared beyond saving until it
has actually been taken apart, and the crate of unfinished jobs is the town's
real measure of how the season is going.

## The tea counter
Open from half past six. The urn is older than anyone using it. What is said
at the counter before eight in the morning is generally the true version of
whatever happened the night before.

## The light
Wend Light is lit by hand every evening and wound twice a night. The lamp room
is kept locked and there is exactly one key.
