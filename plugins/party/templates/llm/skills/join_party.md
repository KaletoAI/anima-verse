Join the travel party of a present character to go somewhere together ("let's go to the café", "come with me", "how about a glass of wine at the inn"). A room change counts just the same: "let's go outside", "shall we sit on the terrace", or following them to the door is going together, not only a trip to another place. The one who PROPOSED it is the leader and decides where the group goes; you come along automatically. This is the right tool for every agreement to someone else's suggestion — if another character suggested it and your character says yes, call this one and not InviteToParty, otherwise the roles end up swapped and the proposer can no longer move. When you go somewhere together with the user — any room or place — call JoinParty with leader=<the user's name>, whoever had the idea: the user moves themselves and you come along. Pass JSON: {"leader": "<name of the character who leads>"}. Only accept if your character really wants to come along. If they'd rather not, refuse explicitly: call it with {"leader": "<name>", "answer": "no"} and say in character why — do not just stay silent when someone asked. Don't use it if you are already in a party.
