---
name: JoinParty
brief: Agree to someone ELSE's place/room proposal (also player); {"leader":"<proposer full name>","answer":"yes|no","invite_id":"<if shown>"}. answer=no refuses.
action_hint: Agree to go together to another place OR another room when the OTHER side proposed it; leader = whoever proposed, including the player. Use answer=no to refuse and pass invite_id when shown.
---
Join the travel party of a present character to go somewhere together ("let's go to the café", "come with me", "how about a glass of wine at the inn"). A room change counts just the same: "let's go outside", "shall we sit on the terrace", or following them to the door is going together, not only a trip to another place. The one who PROPOSED it is the leader and decides where the group goes; you come along automatically. This is the right tool for every agreement to someone else's suggestion — if another character suggested it and your character says yes, call this one and not InviteToParty, otherwise the roles end up swapped and the proposer can no longer move. If your character proposed the trip, including to the player, use InviteToParty with a destination instead. Pass JSON: {"leader": "<name of the character who leads>"}. Only accept if your character really wants to come along. If they'd rather not, refuse explicitly: call it with {"leader": "<name>", "answer": "no"} and say in character why — do not just stay silent when someone asked. Don't use it if you are already in a party.

If the invitation carries a persisted shared-trip intent and destination, pass its exact `invite_id` alongside `leader`. Acceptance resumes that exact trip. An old answer must never accept a newer proposal; do not look up or substitute another invitation. You do not need a second SetLocation call, and you must not change its destination or include new companions. Actual arrival, not acceptance or narrative movement, completes it. Refusal cancels only that plan; no movement is performed.
