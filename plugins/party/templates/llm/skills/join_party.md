---
name: JoinParty
action_hint: Your character AGREES to a shared trip that SOMEONE ELSE proposed (the user included) — leader = the name of whoever proposed it, they lead and your character comes along; use this for every "yes, let's go together", never InviteToParty
---
Join the travel party of a present character who proposed going somewhere together ("let's go to the café", "come with me", "how about a glass of wine at the inn") and whose proposal your character accepts. The one who PROPOSED it is the leader and decides where the group goes; you come along automatically. This is the right tool for every agreement to someone else's suggestion — if the user or another character suggested the trip and your character says yes, call this one and not InviteToParty, otherwise the roles end up swapped and the proposer can no longer move. Pass JSON: {"leader": "<name of the character who proposed the trip>"}. Only call it if your character really wants to come along; if they'd rather not, just answer in-character and don't call it. Don't use it if you are already in a party.
