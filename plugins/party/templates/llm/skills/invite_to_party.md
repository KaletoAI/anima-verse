---
name: InviteToParty
brief: YOU propose going together to a room here or another place to someone present, the player too; {"target":"<exact name of who you ask>","destination":<listed object>}. Other side proposed: JoinParty.
action_hint: Your character PROPOSES going together to a present person, including the player; the proposer leads. Copy a structured destination from the menu. If the OTHER side proposed and your character agrees, use JoinParty with leader=<proposer>.
---
Invite a person, including the player avatar, who is present in your current room to travel together as a party. Use this ONLY when your character is the one making the proposal ("come with me to the café", "let's go, I'll take you there"). The inviter becomes the party LEADER: during the agreed trip the server moves the group to its exact destination, and the invited character cannot move independently. Therefore: if the OTHER person suggested the shared trip and your character just says yes, do NOT invite them back; call JoinParty with leader=<their name> instead, otherwise the one whose idea it was ends up unable to move. A proposal to the player follows the same rule: your character leads and the avatar answers through Join/Decline. Pass JSON: {"target":"<exact full name>","destination":{"location_id":"<known id>","room_id":"<known id>"}} for a proposed destination (another place or just another room here). A plain invitation without a destination uses only target. Do NOT use it for someone who is not present, or who is already in a party.

For a destination-bound agreement, pass a structured `destination={"location_id":"<known id>","room_id":"<optional id>"}` and `scope="pair"` (only you and one new companion) or explicit `scope="party"` (also include every current follower). The server persists one shared-trip intent, obtains the companion's actual answer, resumes that exact destination and completes only after everyone arrives. Never silently leave/dissolve a party to make pair scope fit. To continue an existing shared trip owned by you, pass its `intent_id` and the same target; do not create a replacement plan or independently call SetLocation before consent.

For an undertaking such as going somewhere and then doing something together, also pass `after_arrival={"type":"pair","pose_key":"<exact available paired pose key>"}`. This requires exactly two trip members. Name the destination AND activity in your proposal. The companion consents to the whole proposal; after arrival the normal pair invitation still checks their willingness and availability. The intent completes only when the actual activity ends, not merely when the trip arrives or the activity is requested. Never invent an animation key or execute arbitrary tools in this field.

To answer somebody else's existing destination-bound proposal, prefer JoinParty
with its exact `invite_id`. A matching counter-proposal is normalized to that
answer only when you supply the exact current `intent_id`; a destination alone
does not identify which invitation you are accepting.

Destination-bound consent lasts only for that proposal. If accepting it creates a new party, the added follower leaves automatically when the trip completes, or when it is cancelled, fails or expires. Without an activity this happens on arrival; with an agreed activity it happens at that activity's actual end. A party that already existed before the proposal remains intact. Plain invitations without a destination remain ordinary parties until someone leaves.
