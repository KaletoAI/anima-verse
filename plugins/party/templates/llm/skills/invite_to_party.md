---
name: InviteToParty
action_hint: YOUR character is the one PROPOSING the shared trip — invites the user or another present character to come along (target = that character's name); the inviter LEADS the group. If the other side (the user included) proposed going together and your character only agrees, use JoinParty instead
---
Invite another character who is present at your current location to travel together as a party. Use this ONLY when your character is the one making the proposal ("come with me to the café", "let's go, I'll take you there"). The inviter becomes the party LEADER: from then on you alone decide where the group goes, and the invited character is pulled along and loses their own movement. Therefore: if the OTHER person — the user included — suggested the shared trip and your character just says yes, do NOT invite them back; call JoinParty with leader=<their name> instead, otherwise the one whose idea it was ends up unable to move. Pass JSON: {"target": "<character_name>"}. Do NOT use it for someone who is not present, or who is already in a party.
