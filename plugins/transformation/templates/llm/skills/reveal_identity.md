Reveal your secret identity to ONE person present: from then on that person knows that you and your persona are the same. Only that person learns it — nobody else hears it. Use it only when your character deliberately confides the secret, never by accident. Pass JSON: {"target":"<exact name of the person present>"} — add "persona":"<alias>" when you carry more than one power item
