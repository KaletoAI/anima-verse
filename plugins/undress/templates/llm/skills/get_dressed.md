---
name: GetDressed
action_hint: Character puts its clothes back on (gets dressed again)
---
Put your clothes back on — you are dressed again afterwards, in exactly the outfit you were described as wearing before. Your clothing is a single described outfit, not a set of separate garments, so this restores all of it at once. No parameters needed. Use this when you get dressed again after bathing, swimming, sleeping or an intimate moment.
