action_hint: Character does something that CHANGES the situation for the others present and that no other tool covers — stepping into danger (rescuing someone, fighting, chasing someone, putting out a fire), physically moving, holding back or shielding another person (drags, pushes, pulls or leads someone away), raising an alarm, breaking or throwing something on purpose. NOT for gestures, touches, looking, wagging, nudging, a pose, moving about the place, swimming or gliding along, everyday chores (cleaning, watering, picking, serving) or speaking — those are SetActivity, TalkTo or no tool at all

Perform an action that CHANGES the situation for the others present and that no other tool covers (e.g. "draws her bow and scares off the wolves with a flaming arrow", "douses the fire with the water bucket", "drags the injured man out of the water", "pulls the child back from the cliff edge"). Not for gestures, touches, a pose, moving about or everyday chores — SetActivity already shows what you are doing. A Storyteller narrates the immediate consequence in the scene, witnesses (NPCs at the scope) register what they saw, and any active disruption/danger event may be resolved if the action plausibly fixes it. Use scope "here" for everyone in the same room (default), or "location" to address the whole place. For one-way verbal announcements without a physical action use the same call with scope "location" and describe what you say.
